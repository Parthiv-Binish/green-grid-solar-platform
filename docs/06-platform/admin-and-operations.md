# Admin and Staff Operations

## 1. Role model

- Super administrator.
- Management.
- Sales.
- Surveyor.
- Technical reviewer.
- Installation / project technician.
- Accounts.
- Support.
- Content editor.
- Inventory / procurement.

Roles should be configurable but permissions must be explicit.

## 2. Dashboard

Management metrics:
- New leads by source.
- Lead response time.
- Qualified pipeline.
- Surveys requested / completed.
- Quotations issued / accepted.
- Projects by stage.
- Revenue and receivables (restricted).
- Inventory alerts.
- Support tickets and ageing.

Every metric must have a documented definition and source query.

## 3. CRM

- Lead inbox and filters.
- Assignment and reassignment.
- Duplicate detection.
- Contact history.
- Follow-up tasks.
- Lead status and lost reason.
- Conversion to customer / project.
- Import/export with permission and audit.

## 4. Calendar

- Staff availability.
- Service areas.
- Survey and service appointments.
- Conflict prevention.
- Assignment and rescheduling.
- Calendar views by staff and date.
- Reminder queue.

## 5. Quotation workspace

- Customer and property selection.
- Technical BOM.
- Cost and margin controls.
- Discount approval.
- Terms and validity.
- PDF preview.
- Version history.
- Send / resend and acceptance status.

## 6. Project operations

- Project board by stage.
- Task assignments.
- Procurement and material allocation.
- Installation calendar.
- Checklists and evidence.
- Delay / blocker tracking.
- Commissioning and handover.

## 7. Inventory

- Products and variants.
- Stock by location.
- Serial / batch records.
- Supplier and purchase order.
- Goods receipt.
- Project allocation.
- Returns / damage.
- Stock movement history.
- Reorder threshold.

## 8. Finance

- Payment ledger.
- Receipts.
- Reconciliation.
- Refunds and reversals.
- Outstanding balance.
- Export for accounting.
- Separation of subsidy information from company receipts.

## 9. Support desk

- Ticket queue.
- Priority and category.
- Assignment.
- SLA / ageing.
- Customer conversation.
- Service visit.
- Warranty claim.
- Resolution code and feedback.

## 10. CMS

- Pages and SEO metadata.
- Banners.
- Product visibility.
- Projects and testimonials.
- FAQs.
- Service areas.
- Legal policy content.
- Publish / unpublish workflow.

## 11. Audit and approvals

Log role changes, price changes, quotation approval, payment status changes, project completion, document access where appropriate, and destructive actions. Require confirmation for sensitive operations.
