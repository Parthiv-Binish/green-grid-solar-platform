# Official government, utility, standards and manufacturer logo/asset sources

Research register for Green Grid website and documents. Links point to official organisations or their own media pages wherever available. A website being official does **not** automatically grant permission to reuse its logo. Download only from official media/brand resources, preserve trademark notices, and obtain written permission where required. Do not imply endorsement, empanelment, certification, subsidy approval, or partnership.

**Checked:** 16 November 2026. Re-check each source and its current brand-use terms before publishing.

## F. Logo/asset intake record
For every third-party logo/photo saved locally, record:
- Brand/organisation and exact asset name.
- Official source page and direct asset URL.
- Download date and version.
- Licence/permission or written approval reference.
- Required attribution and trademark notice.
- Allowed placements and prohibited contexts.
- Reviewer and approval date.
- File checksum and dimensions.
