# Delivery Roadmap

## Release 0 — Discovery and validation

Deliverables:
- Confirm legal business identity and contact details.
- Confirm products, models, supplier relationships and warranty sources.
- Resolve advertised pricing mismatch.
- Confirm service areas and booking rules.
- Verify subsidy and vendor-related claims.
- Approve sitemap, brand direction and business processes.
- Approve data and privacy requirements.

Exit criteria: no unresolved critical business fact is represented as a public claim.

## Release 1 — Public website and lead generation

Features:
- Responsive homepage and company pages.
- Solar solution education.
- Verified product catalogue.
- Calculator with transparent assumptions.
- Subsidy guide with official links.
- Projects gallery using approved evidence.
- Contact and enquiry forms.
- Site-assessment booking request.
- Basic admin CMS and lead inbox.

Exit criteria:
- All forms tested.
- Enquiries reach staff.
- Contact information verified.
- Mobile and accessibility review complete.
- No fabricated product or project data.

## Release 2 — Customer and sales workflow

Features:
- Customer authentication.
- Customer portal.
- Booking confirmation and staff calendar.
- Survey checklist.
- Quotation builder and versioning.
- PDF proposal.
- Acceptance workflow.
- Payment ledger and receipts.
- Email / WhatsApp notifications.

Exit criteria:
- Customer record isolation tested.
- Booking conflicts prevented.
- Quotation revisions traceable.
- Payment reconciliation tested.

## Release 3 — Installation and service operations

Features:
- Project tracking.
- Staff assignments.
- Procurement and inventory.
- Commissioning / handover documents.
- Warranty registration.
- Support ticketing and service visits.
- Operational reporting.

Exit criteria:
- Project can be followed from accepted quotation to handover.
- Customer can access only authorised documents.
- Support lifecycle tested.

## Release 4 — Advanced integrations

Potential features:
- Payment gateway.
- Supplier inventory feeds.
- Solar inverter monitoring.
- Advanced route planning.
- Preventive maintenance reminders.
- Customer energy analytics.

Only implement where business value, API access, data quality and support responsibilities are confirmed.

## Release governance

Each release needs:
- Approved scope.
- Acceptance criteria.
- Database migration plan.
- Security review.
- Test evidence.
- Rollback plan.
- User training and operational owner.
