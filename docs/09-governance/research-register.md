# Research Register

## Purpose

Track external claims that must be verified before they become website content or business rules.

| Topic | Primary source to consult | Required verification | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| PM Surya Ghar eligibility and subsidy | https://pmsuryaghar.gov.in/ and https://mnre.gov.in/ | Current eligibility, subsidy amount, caps, process and dates | Pending |
| Vendor registration | National scheme portal / official MNRE guidance | Green Grid's actual status and permitted claims | Pending |
| Kerala rooftop solar process | https://ekiran.kseb.in/ | Current process, documents and timelines | Pending |
| Kerala electricity regulations | KSERC official website | Applicable current orders and regulations | Pending |
| Product specifications | Manufacturer datasheets | Exact model data and revision | Pending |
| Product warranty | Manufacturer warranty documents | Model-specific coverage and exclusions | Pending |
| GST / legal identity | Official registration evidence supplied by business | Name, GSTIN and status | Pending |
| Privacy obligations | Official MeitY / CERT-In sources and legal adviser | Applicable law, commencement and obligations | Pending |
| Solar resource / generation | Documented technical source | Location and modelling assumptions | Pending |

## Research rules

- Prefer official primary sources for law, schemes and utility procedures.
- Record access date and document / page revision.
- Do not infer a company's registration from a directory listing.
- Do not infer authorised dealership from a logo on a brochure.
- Do not infer a product's warranty from a generic brand page.
- Record conflicts and ask the business to resolve them.
- Re-check time-sensitive information immediately before publication.
