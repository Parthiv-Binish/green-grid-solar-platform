# Booking and Site Survey

## 1. Booking types

Initial proposed services:
- Solar consultation.
- Site assessment / roof survey.
- Existing-system inspection.
- Maintenance / service visit.
- Installation appointment (staff-managed after project approval).

Confirm which services are actually offered and whether any appointment is chargeable.

## 2. Booking flow

1. Select service.
2. Enter customer contact details.
3. Enter property address and PIN code.
4. Select preferred date / time window.
5. Provide property and system details.
6. Upload optional bill / roof photographs.
7. Review consent and submit.
8. Receive booking request reference.
9. Staff validates service area, slot and staff capacity.
10. Customer receives confirmed / alternate slot.

## 3. Booking statuses

- Draft.
- Requested.
- Pending confirmation.
- Confirmed.
- Reschedule requested.
- Rescheduled.
- Cancelled by customer.
- Cancelled by business.
- Assigned.
- In progress.
- Completed.
- No-show.

Do not display “confirmed” until a valid slot and staff resource are assigned.

## 4. Scheduling rules

- Business-configured operating hours.
- Holidays and blackout dates.
- Service-area / travel-time constraints.
- Staff skills and availability.
- Appointment duration and buffer.
- Maximum daily appointments.
- Conflict prevention under concurrent booking.
- Reschedule and cancellation cut-off rules.
- Manual override restricted to authorised staff and audited.

## 5. Survey checklist

### Property
- Customer and property reference.
- Address / PIN code.
- Property type and ownership / permission status.
- Roof access and safety constraints.

### Roof
- Roof type and material.
- Approximate usable area.
- Orientation and tilt.
- Shading and obstructions.
- Structural condition.
- Waterproofing / mounting concerns.
- Photographs and measurements.

### Electrical and load
- Connection type and phase.
- Sanctioned load / relevant bill details.
- Main panel / distribution board observations.
- Earthing and protection observations.
- Existing inverter / generator / battery.
- Customer's usage profile and backup requirement.

### Outcome
- Surveyor recommendation.
- Missing information.
- Site constraints.
- Follow-up required.
- Technical review status.

The checklist supports assessment; final design and safety sign-off must be performed by qualified personnel.

## 6. Notifications

Send request receipt, confirmation, reminder, reschedule, cancellation and completion notifications. Use consented channels and avoid including sensitive documents in message bodies.
