# Product Catalogue Requirements

## 1. Purpose

Provide a trustworthy catalogue of equipment that Green Grid actually supplies or can source. The catalogue should educate and support enquiries; it should not imply that every listed item is compatible with every system.

## 2. Categories

- Solar PV modules / panels.
- On-grid inverters.
- Off-grid inverters.
- Hybrid inverters.
- Lithium battery systems.
- Tubular / lead-acid batteries.
- Mounting structures.
- DC and AC protection equipment.
- Cables, connectors and accessories.
- Monitoring and communication devices.

## 3. Brands observed in supplied brochure

The brochure displays Adani Solar, Waaree Solar, Amaron, Luminous, Microtek and V-Guard. These are leads for catalogue discovery only. Confirm current supply relationship, exact product lines and permission to use brand assets.

## 4. Product data model

Required fields:
- Product name.
- Category and subcategory.
- Manufacturer / brand.
- Exact model / SKU.
- Product description.
- Technical specifications by category.
- Datasheet URL / document.
- Product image and image rights.
- Warranty information and source document.
- Availability status.
- Enquiry visibility.
- Last verified date.
- Data source / staff verifier.

Optional commercial fields:
- Indicative price or “request a quote”.
- GST treatment.
- Stock quantity (staff only).
- Lead time.
- Supplier reference.
- Purchase cost (restricted).
- Serial / batch tracking capability.

## 5. Category-specific specifications

### Solar modules
- Rated power (Wp).
- Cell technology.
- Module efficiency.
- Dimensions and weight.
- Voc / Isc / Vmp / Imp.
- Temperature coefficients.
- Maximum system voltage.
- Applicable certifications.
- Product warranty and performance warranty.

### Inverters
- Type: on-grid / off-grid / hybrid.
- Rated AC output.
- Maximum PV input.
- MPPT count and range.
- Battery voltage / chemistry compatibility where applicable.
- Efficiency.
- Grid and backup output behaviour.
- Monitoring capability.
- Warranty.

### Batteries
- Chemistry.
- Nominal voltage.
- Nominal and usable capacity.
- Continuous / peak power.
- Cycle-life conditions.
- Depth-of-discharge assumptions.
- Compatible inverter models.
- Warranty and operating conditions.

### Structures and electrical balance of system
- Material and coating.
- Dimensions / thickness where relevant.
- Wind / structural design basis.
- Cable and connector ratings.
- Protection device ratings.
- Earthing and surge protection details.

## 6. Filters and comparison

Filters may include category, brand, capacity, technology, system type and availability. Comparison must only compare like-for-like fields. Missing specifications should be shown as “Not provided” or omitted, never fabricated.

## 7. Compatibility

A product page must not imply universal compatibility. System configuration and final compatibility must be validated by an authorised technical reviewer.

## 8. Catalogue states

- Draft.
- Pending verification.
- Published.
- Temporarily unavailable.
- Discontinued.
- Archived.

Only verified products may be published. Price and stock should be optional and clearly dated.
