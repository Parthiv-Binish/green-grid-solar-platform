# Project Charter

## 1. Project summary

Create a customer-facing website and connected business operations platform for Green Grid Solar Solutions, a solar solutions business presented in its brochure as operating from the Erattupetta / Aruvithura area of Kerala.

The first release should establish a credible digital presence and convert visitors into qualified enquiries and site-assessment requests. Later releases should connect the customer lifecycle to quotations, installation, documents, warranty and support operations.

## 2. Vision

Make it straightforward for a household or business to understand its solar options, receive a transparent and technically appropriate proposal, and remain informed throughout the relationship with Green Grid.

## 3. Business outcomes

- Increase qualified residential and commercial enquiries.
- Reduce incomplete enquiries and repetitive manual data collection.
- Standardise site-survey and quotation processes.
- Improve lead follow-up visibility.
- Reduce booking conflicts and missed appointments.
- Give customers clear project status and access to documents.
- Create a traceable after-sales and warranty process.
- Give management reliable operational reporting.

No numeric targets should be set until the business provides baseline enquiry, conversion, installation and service data.

## 4. Product boundaries

### Included in the long-term platform vision

- Public marketing website and CMS.
- Solar solutions education.
- Product and brand catalogue.
- Solar savings estimator.
- Subsidy information and official application guidance.
- Lead and enquiry management.
- Consultation and site-survey booking.
- Customer accounts and portal.
- Site survey records and attachments.
- Quotation and proposal generation.
- Project and installation tracking.
- Payment records and receipts.
- Product registration and warranty records.
- Customer support and service tickets.
- Inventory and supplier management.
- Staff roles, assignments and reporting.
- Email / WhatsApp / SMS notifications, subject to provider and consent.
- Optional solar monitoring integrations where supported hardware APIs exist.

### Explicitly not assumed for the first release

- Direct sale and checkout of every solar product.
- Automatic subsidy application on behalf of a customer.
- Guaranteed electricity-bill reduction or payback.
- Live inverter monitoring without verified compatible APIs.
- Automatic KSEB approval or net-metering processing.
- Automated stock synchronisation with suppliers.
- A mobile application.
- AI-based system design or engineering sign-off.

## 5. Success measures

Instrument the platform so the business can measure:
- Website enquiry conversion rate.
- Qualified lead rate.
- Enquiry response time.
- Survey booking and completion rate.
- Quotation turnaround time.
- Quotation acceptance rate.
- Booking reschedule / no-show rate.
- Installation stage duration.
- Project completion rate.
- Outstanding payment ageing.
- Support first-response and resolution time.
- Warranty claim volume and resolution time.
- Customer satisfaction after installation and service.

## 6. Stakeholders

- Business owner / management.
- Sales and enquiry team.
- Technical surveyors / system designers.
- Installation technicians and contractors.
- Accounts / payment reconciliation.
- Customer support.
- Customers and property owners.
- Product suppliers and manufacturers.
- Relevant utility, regulatory and government scheme bodies.

## 7. Constraints and dependencies

- Actual business identity, registrations and contact details need confirmation.
- Product catalogue, models, stock and price lists must be supplied by the business.
- Vendor registration and subsidy-related claims must be verified.
- KSEB and applicable regulatory processes may change.
- Appointment availability depends on staff capacity and service area.
- Payment and messaging integrations require provider accounts and credentials.
- Solar monitoring depends on inverter / battery manufacturer APIs and customer consent.

## 8. Governance

The business owner approves commercial, legal and operational rules. A technical owner approves system specifications and installation workflow. The product owner maintains this documentation and records decisions. No unresolved high-risk claim should be published as fact.
