# Personas and Roles

## 1. Customer personas

### P1 — Residential bill reducer
- Owns or is authorised to make decisions for a house.
- Wants to reduce electricity costs.
- May not understand kW, kWh, export or net-metering.
- Needs a simple estimator, plain-language explanations and a site visit.
- Success: understands a suitable option and receives a transparent quotation.

### P2 — Backup-focused homeowner
- Experiences power interruptions.
- Wants backup for selected household loads.
- Needs a clear explanation of on-grid versus hybrid / off-grid.
- Must understand battery capacity, backup load and runtime limitations.
- Success: receives a system proposal matched to critical loads and backup expectations.

### P3 — Commercial property owner
- Operates a shop, office, institution or commercial building.
- May have higher or daytime loads and more complex roof / electrical conditions.
- Needs a technical assessment and custom proposal.
- Success: receives an auditable proposal with generation and financial assumptions.

### P4 — Existing solar customer
- Has an installed system or product.
- Needs documents, warranty details, service history or repair.
- Success: can identify the system and raise a traceable service request.

## 2. Internal personas

### Sales executive
- Reviews leads, contacts prospects, records requirements and follow-ups.
- Creates and revises quotations within permissions.
- Cannot approve unauthorised discounts or change technical sign-off.

### Surveyor
- Receives assigned appointments.
- Completes site checklist, measurements, photographs and observations.
- Submits findings for technical review.

### Technical designer / engineer
- Reviews survey data and load requirements.
- Selects compatible components and validates design assumptions.
- Approves technical BOM and system configuration.

### Installation technician / project lead
- Views assigned jobs and installation instructions.
- Updates milestones and uploads evidence.
- Records issues and completion checklist.

### Accounts staff
- Records payments, receipts, refunds and reconciliation references.
- Cannot alter technical design or mark a payment as collected without evidence.

### Support agent
- Handles service requests, warranty questions and customer communications.
- Escalates safety, electrical and unresolved issues.

### Administrator
- Manages users, roles, configuration and access.
- Sensitive actions are audited.

### Management
- Reviews performance, pipeline, revenue, project progress and service metrics.

## 3. Permission principles

- Least privilege by default.
- Customers see only their own profile, property, enquiries, quotations, bookings, projects, documents, payments and tickets.
- Staff see only records needed for their assigned role and work.
- Technical approvals are restricted to authorised technical roles.
- Payment reconciliation is restricted to accounts roles.
- Admin actions and changes to commercial or technical records are logged.
- No shared staff accounts.
