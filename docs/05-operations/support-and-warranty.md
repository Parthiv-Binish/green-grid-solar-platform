# Customer Support and Warranty

## 1. Support channels

- Website contact form.
- Customer portal ticket.
- Business phone.
- WhatsApp Business, subject to account setup and consent.
- Email.
- Service visit request.

Publish only verified and monitored contact channels.

## 2. Ticket categories

- System not generating.
- Inverter alarm / fault.
- Battery / backup issue.
- Monitoring / connectivity issue.
- Physical damage / water ingress.
- Cleaning / maintenance.
- Warranty claim.
- Installation workmanship.
- Billing / documentation.
- General enquiry.

## 3. Ticket lifecycle

Open → Triage → Assigned → In progress → Waiting for customer / supplier → Resolved → Closed.

Allow reopening within a configured period. Track first response, resolution, escalation and customer feedback.

## 4. Safety triage

Potential electrical shock, fire, smoke, burning smell, exposed conductors or water-related electrical hazards must be flagged as safety-critical. Give clear advice to keep away from the equipment and contact appropriate emergency / qualified electrical support. Do not ask unqualified customers to open or repair electrical equipment.

## 5. Warranty record

- Customer and project.
- Product manufacturer.
- Model and serial number.
- Purchase / installation / commissioning date.
- Warranty type and duration.
- Warranty document and source.
- Registration reference.
- Coverage exclusions.
- Claim history.
- Claim owner and status.

Do not infer warranty duration from generic brand marketing. Store the exact model-specific warranty document and conditions.

## 6. Service visit

- Linked ticket and installed system.
- Visit date / assigned technician.
- Diagnosis.
- Work performed.
- Parts replaced.
- Customer approval where needed.
- Before / after evidence.
- Service report.
- Follow-up recommendation.
